AERA sees all of it.

A laser particle sensor and a gas sensor sample the room continuously. When something changes, the fan is already moving before you smell it.

Designed to fit beautifully anywhere.

Breathe cleaner.

It handles the traffic of a shared room — cooking, candles, a dog on the sofa — without turning into furniture you have to explain.

Sleep deeper.

Night mode drops the fan to a low hush and dims the display to a single thread of light. Nothing blinks at 3am.

Stay focused.

Stale air makes an afternoon feel longer than it is. AERA keeps the room turning over so the room stops being the problem.

Air, made visible.

One number, lit from inside the housing. Walk past and you know where the room stands. No app, no notification, no decision to make.

Auto from the first breath

Plug it in and it starts reading. There's no setup flow to finish and no mode to remember to switch.

Quiet as the default, not a setting

The fan spends most of its life at the bottom of its range. It only gets loud when the room gives it a reason.

Filter life you can read across the room

The light turns amber when the filter is near the end. That's the whole maintenance story.

Three layers. One pass.

01
Pre-filter

Hair, lint and the dust you can see. It's washable, so the expensive layer behind it doesn't do this job.

02
True HEPA

Pleated glass fibre, sealed at the edges. This is where pollen, dander and smoke particles stop travelling.

03
Activated carbon

The layer that handles smell — last night's dinner, fresh paint, the bin. Particles are only half of clean air.

Air enters at the base, turns once through all three stages, and leaves through the top. One path, no bypass.
